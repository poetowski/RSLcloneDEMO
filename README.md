# The Loom: Reliquary of Legends — an RSL / SWGOH-style hero battler in pixel art

A turn-based hero collector in the style of **RAID: Shadow Legends** and **Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes**, built to answer one question: *can it look and play that well with 2D pixel art?* Every sprite, animation, effect, tile, backdrop, icon, the logo, the world map and the font are **generated by code** in this repo (`npm run art`), from one palette and one light.

> **Status: built from Jakub's own design.** The name and the logo are final: **The Loom: Reliquary of Legends** (code name TLROL), high fantasy with a gothic arcane look. Since 2026-10-10 its content comes from his design record ([docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md](docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md)): the premise of the broken loom, the affinities, rarities, roles and factions with their standards, twenty-four champions (their names, looks and kits), five areas (the Fracture Reach from his working notes and brief, the Verdant Grove's background on his brief, the Moonlit Basin, Earth Scars and the Snowy Mesa named and described by him), the world map, the Weaver Matrix and summoning. What he has not designed yet is marked as a stand-in where it lives: the champions' stats and skill numbers, seven Elites' affinities, the enemies, the stage powers and the placeholder stages, and every detail his descriptions did not name. The engine, the tools and the guardrails stay.

![Battle](docs/images/battle.gif)

- **26 champions** in four factions, each with idle, run, every skill, hurt, death and a victory pose, rendered for both facings:
  - *Azure Crown*: Captain Gareth, the Skirmisher, the Lineholder, the Quartermaster, Emissary Handlings, a proud standard-bearer, the Associate, a mage with a staff of white motes, and the Paladin, the first Heroic, a young knight in silver plate with a double-cross shield and a burning sword.
  - *Sanguine Dominion*: the Rime Scholar, the Dominion Medic, the Bloodhound (the first on four legs), the Enforcer, Red Cloak, a bald knight in plate with a silver zweihander, the Black Rhino, a great black bull with prosthetic tusks, and Chief Engineer CJ, a Heroic, a fat forge-master in an orange helm with a sparking two-handed hammer.
  - *Court of Root*: the Follower, a grey wolf, the Walking Bush, a small ent of a bush, the Wild Speaker, a tattooed man with a long stick, the Jungle Herder, a hunchbacked herder with a crook and a bell, the Grove Keeper, an old druid with antlers, and Big Ears, a small big-eared creature with a blowpipe.
  - *Ashveil Reign*: the Skeleton, a short skeleton with a rusty sword, the Rot Hulk, a big, fat abomination of patched flesh, the Bellringer, a very tall skeleton with a golden bell, the Necromancer, a hooded man in black with a book of the dead, the Automated Soul, a headless archer skeleton in azure and gold plate, and Irving Wellbington, a dwarf skeleton with a horned helmet behind a big round shield.
- **Five combat backgrounds**, one for each area, alive with calm motion:
  - the *Fracture Reach*: dawn in autumn after a raid, a haystack burning, a broken windmill, bulky floating islands and a rift torn in the cosmos;
  - the *Verdant Grove*: a jungle ruin at midday below a table mountain, a wall carved with a feathered serpent, macaws and butterflies;
  - the *Moonlit Basin*: night under the moon, glowing moss, glass reeds and tide-pools, now and then a shooting star;
  - *Earth Scars*: the steppe by day, yurts with chains of flags on very long poles, wild yaks, two snowy peaks and a zeppelin;
  - the *Snowy Mesa*: a snowy tabletop mountain before sunset among many more, joined by cables hung with candles, a castle breathing purple dust, the arbiter's statue with his sword and a snowstorm coming on.
- **A campaign** of five areas on a painted world map three screens wide that grows east area by area: from a rift in the west through the Fracture Reach's village in its autumn fields, the forest of the Verdant Grove, the Moonlit Basin under its own night and the steppe of Earth Scars with its two ravines, to the brink beyond which the Snowy Mesa's tabletop mountains rise out of a sea of cloud. One node per area opens its list of stages (drag the map, step between areas with the arrows, or press `M` for the whole world). Zone 1 holds 10 stages; Zones 2 to 5 hold 10 placeholder stages each, to see and test them. The second champion joins after the first battle; every other champion is summoned.
- **A Weaver Matrix** for every champion: six named slots in two triangles holding Thread Spools, each with a main stat and strands, and eight Weave Patterns that two spools of a kind weave, on the champion page's MATRIX tab.
- **Weaving**, the storage of every spool: spools drop from every campaign stage, each area holding two Weave Patterns, and wait here as cards in groups by pattern, one grade per row, filtered by slot and by state; choose one to read it, weave it into a champion (the champion, then the slot, with a preview of the stats it changes and the patterns it completes), take it out or unravel it. New finds wear the NEW badge.
- **A main menu that is a place**: the Sanctum, an island adrift under the broken celestial Loom, its golden rim cracked open over a ringed planet, threads of starlight hanging from it and its cloth streaming from the break; you go on by clicking its places (the Starbridge Gate, the Reliquary, the Weaver's Spire, the Observatory, an astrolabe), and a different few of your champions stand on its plaza each time.
- **Challenges**: the Herald's Board on its own drifting rock leads to quests in three tabs, Daily, Persistent and the **Journey**, a pathway across the screen where the Loom's thread runs through a knot for every quest, woven gold as each is claimed: claim one to open the next.
- **A tutorial for new accounts**, made with driver.js: the screen dims round the spot to click, gold corners breathe round it and a gloved hand points at it, while a card in the game's own pixel font says why; it leads from the Sanctum through the campaign and a first battle (the turn meter, a skill, a target, a cooldown) to the Challenges, where finishing it is the Journey's first quest. It can be skipped, and played again from the Journey. A later lesson walks the first summon, once its Stardust is in.
- **Summoning at the Wishing Star**: every victory finds Stardust, 100 Stardust merge into a Soulstar, and a Soulstar summons a champion you do not have yet: 70% Common, 25% Elite, 5% Heroic. Wish for a faction and a role first and the champions it names grow likelier (never certain); the Wishing Star, hung in the Sanctum's sky on a thread of the Loom, shows the odds, your wish's chance and every champion still scattered, and a summon draws the light in, turns it the colour of the rarity that answers and opens on the champion's ceremony.
- **A collection** of champions in groups by faction, the rarest first and one rarity per row, filtered by affinity and by class, with locked silhouettes, and a detail page with every animation and skill.
- **The Academy**: an in-game codex that teaches combat, the turn meter, damage, affinities, all buffs, debuffs and special mechanics, with live demonstrations.
- **Real mechanics**: turn meter, A1/A2/A3 cooldowns, 15 statuses, the affinity cycle (Ember beats Bloom, Bloom beats Tide, Tide beats Ember), accuracy against resistance for debuffs, counterattacks, dispels, lifesteal, execute, turn meter boosts, passives (Undying, Overdrive, Rebuke, which answers a critical hit, stat passives that raise a stat in every battle, Shields given to the frailest at the end of a turn, to an ally just struck or to the struck one himself, a crit that grows as allies fall, Poison answering a blow, Regen answering a debuff, and Taunt with a heal answering a critical hit that leaves its holder standing), a cleanse, a blow that heals its user, a buff placed before the blow, a fallen ally raised, bosses, stars, auto battle and speed controls.
- **Guardrails** that keep new content consistent: balance norms and a balance simulator, content tests, an art audit, and two project skills for adding champions and backgrounds.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open http://localhost:5173. The sprite gallery (every animation, effect and icon) is at http://localhost:5173/gallery.html.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| mouse | everything: click skills, targets and buttons; hover anything for a tooltip |
| `1` `2` `3`, arrows, `Enter` | pick a skill, cycle targets, confirm |
| `A`, `S`, `Esc` | auto battle, speed x1/x2/x3, pause menu (and back in menus) |
| campaign map | drag to move; arrows jump between areas; `W` `A` `S` `D` or `Shift` + arrows slide the map; `M` shows the whole world |
| arrows + `Enter` in menus | move between buttons, activate |

Deep links: `?screen=campaign|team|battle|collection|champion|weaving|academy|challenges|options|recruit|summon`, `&tab=daily`, `?tutorial=1` (start the tutorial; `0` keeps it and its lessons away from a fresh game, `summon` starts the lesson on the first summon), `?stardust=250`, `?soulstars=2` (set what the save holds), `&stage=1-5`, `&team=captain_gareth,rime_scholar`, `&champion=rime_scholar`, `&chapter=buffs`; `?demo=<skill_id>` loops one skill; `&zone=zone5` (a battle or team select on another area's background), `&hp=0.1` (scales the enemies' max HP); `?unlockall=1`, `?progress=1-4` (clear up to a stage), `?reset=1`, `?spools=1` (a sample stock of Thread Spools, added once), `?skyflash=1` (a zone's rare flash in its sky every few seconds).

## The world

The campaign is a line of areas crossed from left to right, each a node on the map that opens its list of stages:

- **I. Fracture Reach** (Zone 1) follows Jakub's working notes: ten stages in the arc contact, the settlement, escalation, the turning point and leaving (the notes are not locked). Its enemies are copies of the two starters until enemies are designed; the first clear of 1-1 brings the Rime Scholar, the only champion a stage brings.
- **II. Verdant Grove** (Zone 2): ten placeholder stages on its jungle-ruin background, painted on his brief, so the area can be tested in game.
- **III. Moonlit Basin** (Zone 3), named and described by Jakub: ten placeholder stages on its night background, walking his description from the grove's roots to the eastern hills.
- **IV. Earth Scars** (Zone 4), named and described by Jakub: ten placeholder stages on its steppe by day, walking his description from the ravines to the zeppelin in its sky.
- **V. Snowy Mesa** (Zone 5), named and described by Jakub: ten placeholder stages on the snowy top of a tabletop mountain before sunset, walking his description from the mesa's top to the arbiter with his sword.

From Zone 2 on the stages' enemies are copies of the roster's champions. Enemies and stage powers are stand-ins until Jakub dictates them. Every victory finds Stardust for the Wishing Star: 20 in the Fracture Reach and 5 more in each area beyond it, 10 more when nobody falls. The map grows east area by area; the overview (`M`) shows the whole world on one screen.

## How the art is made

`tools/art` is a small procedural pixel-art studio:

1. **Rig** (`rig.ts`): a 2D skeleton with FK and 2-bone IK. Poses are key frames; idle and run are parametric cycles.
2. **Shapes** (`parts.ts`, `champions/*.ts`): tapered capsules, domes, bevelled polygons and ribbons build limbs, armour, cloth, wings and weapons.
3. **Renderer** (`render.ts`): rasterizes at native resolution with no anti-aliasing, lights every pixel from one top-left key light, quantizes into 6-step hue-shifted palette ramps, removes orphan pixels, then draws separation lines and coloured outlines.
4. **Effects, zones, UI, map, font** (`fx/`, `zones/`, `ui/`, `map.ts`, `font.ts`) follow the same palette and light.
5. **Build** (`build.ts`): trims and packs every frame of both facings into atlases with hit-frame and event metadata. Deterministic: running it twice produces identical files.
