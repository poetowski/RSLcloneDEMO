# The Loom: Reliquary of Legends — guide for coding agents

A turn-based hero battler (RAID / SWGOH style) in TypeScript + Canvas. All art is generated by code in `tools/art` into `public/assets` (versioned). Quality bar: every change is reviewed visually, in screenshots, before it is done.

## Content approval

The game is **The Loom: Reliquary of Legends** (code name TLROL), high fantasy with a gothic arcane look: no science fiction, no literal yarn or craft look. Approved so far (2026-10-08):

- the name; "Reliquary of Legends" is the subtitle, and heroes stay *champions*;
- the domain lexicon: Master Archivist, Reliquary, Hero Soul File, Weaver Matrix, Thread Spool, Weave Pattern ([docs/DDD.md](docs/DDD.md) 2.6); the Spindle of Fate is a placeholder name with no meaning yet;
- the logo: a gothic rose window (six jewelled lobes, as the Weaver Matrix holds six spools) before THE LOOM in cast gold, with RELIQUARY OF LEGENDS beneath in the `display` face;
- the gear system (2026-10-10): the two-triangle Weaver Matrix, spools with a main stat (Ashen, Silver, Gilded) and strands, attunement, the MATRIX tab and the spool art, and the combat rules that make DEF divide damage (40 / DEF) and critical hits deal double ([docs/MECHANICS_GUIDE.md](docs/MECHANICS_GUIDE.md) 5 and 14);
- the Weaver Matrix outline: six slots whose stat nodes are fixed (one stat) or variable (one of a list); 2- and 4-piece Weave Patterns counted wherever their spools sit; a 2-piece pattern can be woven up to three times;
- **his design decisions** (2026-10-10), recorded in [docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md](docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) and built: the premise (a celestial loom broke; the player is an Archivist who recollects champions, fragmented souls of legends, to repair it; floating islands), the locked enums (affinities Ember > Bloom > Tide > Ember, no Void; rarities Common, Elite, Heroic, Mythic; roles Tank, Damage, Support; factions Azure Crown, Sanguine Dominion, Court of Root, Ashveil Reign, independent of each other), the factions and their standards (a gold crown on blue, a red hexagon on black, a brown claw on green, one white bone across a dark grey), the two starting champions (an Elite Ember Damage warrior of the Azure Crown from the start; an Elite Tide Support of the Sanguine Dominion who joins after the first battle) and Zone 1 from his campaign notes, which are **working notes, not locked**.

Not defined yet: the Weave Patterns (names, rules and values), where spools and materials come from, and everything DESIGN_DECISIONS.md section 7 lists (affinity, rarity, role and faction mechanics, kits, the champion schema, the art pipeline, the starters' names, Zone 2 and later). Until Jakub designs them, stand-ins fill the gaps, each marked as such where it lives: the two champions' names ("Azure Warrior", "Sanguine Support", title "name to come"), kits, stats and bodies (the proof of concept's berserker and sun priestess); Zone 1's enemies (copies of the two); its stage names and texts (quoted from his notes); the size of an affinity edge (±20%) and the rarity budgets (carried over); a first take on Zone 1's background and the world map; the Academy text, rule numbers, norms and effects. Do not add champions, factions, zones or lore beyond his record: he mediates content himself. Jakub, the game's designer, approves or rejects every piece of real content before it is built: names, stats, numbers, mechanics, texts and art.

- Propose first: options with a recommendation, and rendered previews or captures for anything visual. Then wait for his answer.
- Never build unapproved content, also in a session he is not part of: stop at the proposal.
- An approval covers exactly what was shown; a later change to it needs a new yes.
- Implementation inside an approved design (code structure, tests, tooling) needs no separate sign-off.

## Commands

| Command | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `npm run dev` | game at http://localhost:5173 (gallery at `/gallery.html`) |
| `npm test` | rules (`tests/battle.test.ts`), content guardrails and the save (`tests/content.test.ts`), the Reliquary (`tests/reliquary.test.ts`) |
| `npm run balance` | stat budgets, kit norms, win-rate impact, campaign curve; all lines must say `ok` |
| `npm run art` | regenerate assets (`-- champions <id>`, `-- zones <id>`, `-- fx`, `-- ui`, `-- map`, `-- font`) |
| `npm run audit` | art audit: palette, heights, frame box, literals, color budgets |
| `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build` | types for src and tools; production build |
| `npx tsx tools/shots.ts "<url>" <out> "w2500,B1:0"` | screenshots; `B<n>:<ms>` bursts the raw 640x360 buffer |

## Where things are

- Rules: `src/game/battle/` (pure, tested). Content data: `src/game/data/` (champions, statuses, meta, campaign, zones, codex, norms).
- Player state: `src/game/archivist.ts` (the Master Archivist: the save, its migration, unlocks) and `src/game/reliquary/` (Hero Soul Files, the Weaver Matrix, Thread Spools and their strands, the Weave Pattern evaluator); the matrix catalog is `src/game/data/matrix.ts`, its art `tools/art/ui/matrix.ts`, its tab in `src/game/screens/champion.ts`.
- Presentation: `src/game/view/` (battle scene, units, fx, HUD, zone), `src/game/screens/` (menus), `src/game/ui/ui.ts` (UI kit), `src/game/app.ts` + `src/main.ts` (router, deep links).
- Art: `tools/art/champions/` (one module per champion), `tools/art/fx/` (kit + effect groups), `tools/art/zones/` (one module per background + `shared.ts` contract), `tools/art/ui/`, `tools/art/map.ts`, `tools/art/palette.ts` (every color).
- Docs: Jakub's design record `docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md` (the source for content: read it before touching champions, factions or the campaign); `docs/ART_GUIDE.md`, `docs/MECHANICS_GUIDE.md`, `docs/GAME_STRUCTURE.md`, `docs/UI_GUIDE.md`; design intent and measured baseline in `docs/MDA.md`; domain language, invariants and change playbooks in `docs/DDD.md`.

## Skills

- New or reworked champion, enemy, boss or kit: use the **new-champion** skill (`.claude/skills/new-champion/`).
- New or reworked battle background / zone / location: use the **new-combat-background** skill (`.claude/skills/new-combat-background/`).

## Rules

- One key light, top-left, for everything; sprites are rendered for both facings, never mirrored.
- No color literals in champion modules: colors come from `MAT`, `ACCENT`, `INK` in `palette.ts`.
- Numbers stay inside `src/game/data/norms.ts`; tune stage `power` with `npm run balance`.
- A rule change updates its test, `docs/MECHANICS_GUIDE.md` and the Academy text in `src/game/data/codex.ts` together.
- A change to mechanics, content or the domain model fills in the MDA impact card (`docs/MDA.md` section 7) and keeps `docs/DDD.md` true (glossary, invariants, drift register).
- Skill `hits` match the animation's hit frames; effect names must exist (content tests check both).
- Regenerate and commit `public/assets` with the code that needs it.
- Pixel art only: integer coordinates, no smooth gradients or anti-aliased shapes (use `ui.bands`, `ui.glow`, dithering).
- Text uses the four faces of the one pixel font: `regular`, `bold`, `display` for titles, names in headers and big numbers, and `micro` digits for counters on status icons; never scale `regular` or `bold` up. A new character in any text needs a glyph (content tests check).
